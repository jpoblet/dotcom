---
title: Weaknesses and how I work best
---

## Impatience

My biggest weakness is that I'm very impatient. It's partly why I moved from industrial design to digital in the first place: if something needs to happen, I see no reason for it to wait. At big companies with a lot of layers, improvements can move very slowly, and that can get frustrating.

That said, there's always a reason why things take time, and part of the beauty is letting things evolve gradually. I work every day at being less impatient about results and more welcoming of a calmer pace.

## What a manager might find hard

It hasn't happened to me so far, but if I were my own manager, I'd probably get annoyed that I bring more ideas to the table than are needed. Sometimes I lack the focus to narrow down and commit to one direction, because I want to make sure what we work on is the real game changer.

Something else that describes me well: if I believe in something, I'll put 1000% of myself into it. If I don't, I'll always go looking for more context, to understand the real point and objectives behind a new feature, a change of strategy or even a simple daily task. I motivate myself much more easily when I believe in what I'm doing.
