From a young age, I was fascinated by building and creating. One of my favourite childhood toys was a LEGO set, a gateway into the world of making, imagining and iterating. That curiosity eventually grew into a career, but it was always accompanied by another constant in my life: sport.

## Sport

I started playing roller hockey at the age of three, and I've barely missed a season since. The team sport culture (the camaraderie, the discipline, the strategic thinking) has been part of me for as long as I can remember. At 13, I won the Spanish National Championships. Years later, after moving to Australia, I had the honour of representing the country at four World Championships. One of my proudest moments came in 2018, when I was part of the team that won the Asian Championships in Namwon, South Korea, wearing the Green and Gold.

As a kid, roller hockey shared the stage with alpine skiing. Over time hockey took the lead, but the love for movement and competition never left. I still play in the veterans' roller hockey league, and I've also embraced endurance sports: trail running, triathlons, half marathons and local races. It all started at Melbourne's Elwood Beach, where I did my first triathlon with Tri-Alliance, a local club that got me hooked. Sport has never been just a hobby for me. It's a source of balance, discipline and identity.

## Lanzarote and Chiara

From November 2020 to April 2021, I lived in Lanzarote, escaping the Covid restrictions in Barcelona. It was one of the best times of my life: working remotely, surfing almost every day and enjoying the freedom of an island with relaxed restrictions. I found a great group of friends there, and I'm still in touch with them today.

It's also where I met my wife, Chiara, on 9 April 2021. Our paths crossed at a moment when we were both looking for the same thing: a companion for the journey. She's from Sicily, in love with everything, and she brings me peace in moments of stress. She's lived in Barcelona since 2016, so she understands my culture and speaks my language perfectly. I've picked up Italian, and some Sicilian, because of her. Learning about her culture is a way of knowing her a little more every day. She is the most beautiful person in the world.

We got married on 12 August 2022 in Pontós, in the Empordà, in the middle of a forest, with all our family and lots of friends. We wanted it to feel like a small festival, with music, food and non-stop celebration. We even played: me on guitar and Chiara singing. I designed the brand and website for the wedding too.

Chiara also has her own art project, Sottobosco, for which I created the name, the logo and the brand, and helped direct the website and merch design.
