---
title: How AI has changed the way I work
---

AI has changed the way I look at work, in two ways.

First, it's a great research and context tool. I used to spend weeks reading up on features, investigating technologies, and testing and trying things before I even started drawing a concept. Now a lot of that can happen in one prompt. Planning the development of a product happens much faster too, so I get into "execution mode" sooner.

Second, execution itself. An entrepreneurial designer like me is no longer blocked by a lack of development skills: AI is a great co-pilot, as long as you have the will to learn and know how to guide it. Everybody has ideas, but the execution depends on you: how well you know the tools, and how well defined your user experience and product are.

One thing I feel strongly about: AI still can't have ideas on its own. Only someone with your life experiences, hobbies, skills, network and stories can have them. It's a great tool, a booster for profiles like mine, but a tool nonetheless, like sketching, or wireframing in Figma. I have a toolbox, and I pick the right tool for the job.
