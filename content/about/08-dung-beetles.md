---
title: Why my favourite animal is the dung beetle
---

The dung beetle is my favourite animal because it represents a lot of the qualities I see in myself. I was lucky enough to see them in the wild in Africa, on the savannah, and it was a precious moment. The more I learnt about them, the more I realised how much they behave like entrepreneurs. I've written an article about it, called "Entrepreneurs and Dung Beetles share a common ancestor". It isn't published yet, but I'm planning to put it on Medium.

Dung beetles feed on dung. They make a ball of it, sometimes ten times their own size, and roll it away looking for the perfect place to bury it. The ancient Egyptians considered them sacred and painted them, balls and all, on the walls of their tombs and temples.

In the article I list eight qualities that successful entrepreneurs share, and how the beetle has every one of them:

- **Relentless:** beetles find their way using magnetic fields, the moon and even the Milky Way, and they don't let anything stop them.
- **Resilient:** bigger beetles sometimes steal the ball from smaller ones. The smaller beetle immediately goes looking for a new pile to start again.
- **Insanely obsessed:** find dung, make a ball, roll it. All day, all night. It's a lifetime mission, and very little else crosses their minds.
- **Dreamer:** they probably die feeling they could have built a bigger, better ball. (Not scientifically proven.)
- **Leader:** in mating season, males lead females to the areas where the soil is soft enough to bury the ball, so a new generation can emerge.
- **Believer:** the beetle pushes the ball backwards, so it can't see where it's going. It trusts its senses, and its luck, that the ball will get there.
- **Not afraid of failure:** it knows it might roll down a hill, hit a wall or get stuck in tall grass. It carries on anyway, confident it'll find a way.
- **Creative:** "I need to take this dung with me. What if I make it a ball so it's easier to move, and a huge one so I don't have to find more every other day?"

The ball is your idea, your project or your product. You carry it everywhere and guard it, even while you sleep. Sometimes you roll it the wrong way out of fear or inexperience, and sometimes a bigger beetle takes it, which teaches you how to stop that happening again. Young beetles start with small, manageable balls and build bigger, stronger ones as they grow. Start-ups work the same way. Resilience, creativity, determination, belief, leadership and a healthy dose of obsession will get you across the savannah.
