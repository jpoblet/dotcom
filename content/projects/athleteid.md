## What it is

Anyone who enters trail races, triathlons or marathons knows the routine: the same long registration form, over and over, for every race. With AthleteID, athletes fill in their profile once. A Chrome extension then fills race registration forms on any website, and races that add the "Register with AthleteID" button take the athlete's details in one click.

On the other side, organisers get an admin to run their events: entries, divisions, course and race details, and a public page for each event they can share or embed on their own site.

## How it's built

I designed and built the whole product: the athletes' app, the organisers' admin, the landing page, the extension and the design system they share. It's available in English, Spanish, Catalan and Italian. It comes from my own life as an athlete: I enter a lot of races, and I was tired of typing the same details every time.
