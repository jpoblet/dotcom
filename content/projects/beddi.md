## What it is

Beddi was the website for our wedding, on 12 August 2022 at Mas Terrats in Pontós, Girona. Chiara is Sicilian and I'm Catalan, so everything on it is written in both languages.

## What it did

Guests found everything they needed in one place: who we are, the date and time, the venue, the party and the ceremony, where to sleep (including tipis in the woods next to the venue) and the dress code. It was also how everyone confirmed they were coming, with the RSVP built into the site.

## The design

I designed the brand and built the site myself. The logo is a camper van with our names on either side, a nod to how much we love travelling together in ours.
