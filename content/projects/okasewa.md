## How it started

During my overland trip across Africa, I spent a while living and working on Okasewa, a ranch near Witvlei in Namibia. The original idea was simple: an online catalogue of their pinto horses, so buyers could see every horse in one place. I took the photos of the horses myself.

## What it became

Once I was living there, it was clear the ranch had a lot more to tell. The site grew to show the life and the other activities of the ranch: their beautiful herd of Brahman cattle, the vegetable crop, and the incredible location on rich Namibian soil.

Designing and building a site while living the place it describes was a great experience.
